WHO I AM

About Me

I'm a Telecommunications Engineering student at Università della Calabria, with a background in Computer Engineering. I'm passionate about the intersection of embedded systems, cloud infrastructure, and artificial intelligence.

My work spans the full stack of modern IoT architectures — from writing firmware on an ESP32 to deploying Vision-Language Models on AWS GPU instances. In my bachelor's thesis I built a multi-sensor IoT network (ESP32 + DHT22, GPS, anemometer) with MQTT, a Node-RED dashboard, and real network performance analysis over 4G/5G NSA using Wireshark.

For my current master's project, as part of a four-person team, I evaluated PaliGemma 2 on FER+ for group emotion recognition. Fine-tuning proved counterproductive due to the model's pre-training alignment; the base model with a minimal prompt outperformed all QLoRA checkpoints — accuracy 0.691 (8-class) and 0.695 (4-class distress, macro F1 0.691).

I enjoy working where hardware meets software — understanding a system at the protocol level, then building and measuring it in the real world.
